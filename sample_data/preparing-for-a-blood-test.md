# Preparing for a blood test

Blood tests are taken in the Phlebotomy department on the ground floor of the Green Zone, next to the main outpatient reception.

## Opening times and booking

Phlebotomy is open Monday to Friday from 07:30 to 17:30, and on Saturday from 08:00 to 12:00. It is closed on Sundays and bank holidays.

Most blood tests are walk-in, no appointment needed. Bring your blood test form. The quietest times are usually between 14:00 and 16:00; the busiest is the first hour after opening.

Glucose tolerance tests and tests for children under twelve must be booked in advance on 020 7946 0155.

## Fasting

Fasting means nothing to eat or drink except plain water. Do not chew gum, and do not drink tea, coffee, or juice.

You need to fast for twelve hours before a fasting glucose test or a cholesterol and lipid profile. You do not need to fast for a full blood count, thyroid function tests, kidney or liver function tests, or an HbA1c test. If your form does not say and you are not sure, telephone 020 7946 0155 rather than guessing, because arriving unfasted for a fasting test means coming back another day.

Keep taking your regular medicines while fasting unless your doctor has told you to stop.

## What to bring

Bring the blood test form given to you by your doctor and some form of identification with your name and date of birth. Without the form we cannot take your blood, because the form tells the laboratory which tests to run.

## During the test

Taking blood takes about five minutes. Tell the phlebotomist if you have ever fainted during a blood test, if one arm should be avoided because of a previous mastectomy or a dialysis fistula, or if you are taking blood-thinning medicine.

Drinking a glass of water half an hour before helps, unless you are fasting.

## Getting your results

Most results reach the requesting doctor within three working days. Some specialist tests take up to three weeks. The laboratory cannot give results over the telephone, and the phlebotomist who takes your blood does not see them.

## Children

A parent or guardian must be present for anyone under sixteen. Numbing cream can be applied but needs forty-five minutes to work, so arrive early and ask at reception when you check in.
