# Departments and how to find them

The hospital is arranged in four colour-coded zones. Every corridor junction has a coloured sign, and the floor markings match. If you are lost, the volunteers at the main entrance in blue tabards will walk you there.

## Main entrance and reception

The main entrance is on Riverside Road and is open twenty-four hours. The main reception desk is staffed from 07:00 to 20:00.

## Blue Zone — surgery and inpatients

Level 1 holds the operating theatres and the surgical admissions lounge. Level 2 holds surgical wards B1 to B4. Level 3 holds the intensive care unit and the high dependency unit.

## Green Zone — outpatients and diagnostics

The ground floor holds outpatient reception, phlebotomy for blood tests, and the pharmacy. Level 1 holds Radiology, which covers X-ray, ultrasound, CT, and MRI. Level 2 holds cardiology, respiratory medicine, and the endoscopy unit.

## Amber Zone — emergency and acute care

The Emergency department has its own entrance on Mill Lane, separate from the main entrance, and is open twenty-four hours a day, every day. Ambulances arrive at the rear. The urgent treatment centre for minor injuries sits beside it and is open from 08:00 to 22:00.

## Violet Zone — women, children, and maternity

The ground floor holds the children's outpatient clinic. Level 1 holds the maternity unit and the delivery suite. Level 2 holds the children's ward and the neonatal unit.

## Cafe, shop, and cash machine

The cafe on the ground floor of the Green Zone is open 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays and 09:00 to 17:00 at weekends. The shop next to the main entrance is open 08:00 to 18:00 daily. There is a cash machine in the main entrance foyer.

## Quiet room and chapel

The multi-faith quiet room is on the ground floor of the Blue Zone and is open at all times. Chaplains of several faiths are available; ask any nurse to contact them for you, at any hour.

## Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hospital. Connect to the network named Hospital-Guest and accept the terms. No password is needed.
